About Temp-Humid


Temp-Humid is a platform dedicated to monitoring temperature and humidity data.

We can read temperature and humidity data from DHT11 sensor and upload it to a ThingSpeak cloud using Arduino Uno and ESP8266-01 module.


Our team comprises experts in IoT, and software development, all working together to bring you a cutting-edge solution for mobile robot control.
